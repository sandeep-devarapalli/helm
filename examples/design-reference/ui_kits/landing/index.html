FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is helm?

helm is an agent-operated trading studio. You describe strategies in plain language; the Hermes agent researches, backtests, and runs them live through your connected broker or exchange — US equities, Indian equities (NSE/BSE), and crypto — inside limits you set.

How is this different from a trading bot?

Bots execute a fixed script. Hermes shows its work — every data pull and backtest is visible — and it keeps improving strategies against live fills, drafting each change for your approval before it ships.

Can Hermes trade without my approval?

No. Every strategy runs inside a mandate you commit — universe, max order size, daily cap, leverage — and any refinement is drafted as a diff. Nothing is written until you approve it.

Is anything helm says investment advice?

No. helm is a research and automation tool, not a broker or adviser. Its output is generated by AI, can be wrong, and is not a personal recommendation — verify independently before you rely on it.
